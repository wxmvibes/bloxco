Our Story

Read about the journey of Bloxco Stores.

1
Version 1

Humble Beginnings

Released: March 22, 2022

It all started with a tiny convenience store based in York. The store is pretty small compared to the versions that followed it. The store was branded as "Bloxco Express" and took a lot of inspiration from Tesco, one of the UK's biggest retailers. It had a model of mostly self checkouts and very isolated service. The store received multiple upgrades and renovations before being replaced with Version 2.

2
Version 2

The Game Changer

Released: January 20, 2023

Version 2 marked a significant turning point for Bloxco. The store itself was a massive upgrade from Version 1 with a bigger store and more advanced systems. Multiple popular and trendy products were added to the game, such as the infamous hydration drink PRIME. The item's introduction was a massive success, with the group gaining hundreds of new players joining to try the drink.

3
Version 3

Perfecting the Formula

Released: August 16, 2023

The period of development between v2 and v3 is regarded as a time of major growth for the group. It allowed us to perfect the formula and properly find our footing within the industry, allowing us to offer a unique shopping experience for our customers while also being one of the largest ro-stores on the platform. The development team focused on community feedback, creating a more immersive and enjoyable experience for all players.

4
Version 4

Building the Future

Released: December 21, 2024

Version 4 marked the pinnacle of our store releases at the time. Expanding on the strong groundwork laid by Version 3, it brought a host of innovative features that redefined what could be achieved in the ro-store industry. From an eclectic mix of products, including fish and phones, to new systems like staff tipping and a fully outfitted cafe, it solidified our reputation as the most advanced and forward-thinking store in the industry.

5
Version 5

The Present Day

Released: December 22, 2025

Version 5 represents the latest and greatest store we have released to date. Building on the solid foundation set by Version 4, this store introduced brand new features that push the boundaries of what's possible in the ro-store industry. With products ranging from fish to phones, features like tipping staff members and a fully equipped cafe with custom systems, it has cemented our place as the most technologically advanced store in the industry.
