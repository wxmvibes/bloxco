Rank Structure

Below is the list of ranks in our group, followed by a description and how to obtain this rank.

General Members

myBloxco Member

Normal player in our group.

How to obtain

Join our Roblox group to become a member.

Trainee

Player who has passed an application at the Application Centre. They must now train until they are on our staffing team.

How to obtain

Pass an application at the Application Centre.

Staff Team

Store Colleague

Deals with tills and customer issues.

How to obtain

Obtained by passing a training.

Senior Store Colleague

Deals with tills, customer issues and makes sure Store Colleagues know what they are doing.

How to obtain

Obtained by being promoted from a promotional shift.

Store Security

Deals with trollers, stealers and any customer issues.

How to obtain

Obtained by purchasing the rank via our game passes or training to become it.

Senior Security

Deals with trollers, stealers, customer issues and makes sure Security know what they are doing.

How to obtain

Obtained by being promoted from a promotional shift.

Management Team

Team Leader

Hosts shifts, deals with any issues in-game, and supervises lower ranked staff.

How to obtain

Obtained by being promoted from Senior Store Colleague in a promotional shift.

Assistant Manager

Hosts shifts, deals with any issues in-game, and supervises lower ranked staff.

How to obtain

Obtained by purchasing the rank via our game passes or being promoted from Team Leader.

Store Manager

Hosts shifts, deals with any issues in-game, and supervises lower ranked staff.

How to obtain

Obtained by being promoted from Assistant Manager in a promotional shift.

Area Manager

Hosts shifts, deals with any issues in-game, and supervises lower ranked staff.

How to obtain

Obtained by being promoted from Store Manager in a promotional shift.

Regional Manager

Hosts shifts, deals with any issues in-game, and supervises lower ranked staff.

How to obtain

Obtained by being promoted from Area Manager in a promotional shift.

Booster

Helps manage staff and deals with customer issues and manages the tills.

How to obtain

Obtained by boosting our Discord Server.

Corporate Team

Intern Corporate

Hosts trainings, moderates the game and Discord, and deals with lower ranked staff.

How to obtain

Obtained by being promoted from Store Manager or passing an application.

Junior Corporate

Moderates Discord and Roblox, deals with departmental tasks and answers tickets, also hosts training and shifts.

How to obtain

Obtained by being promoted from Intern Corporate / passing intern trial.

Senior Corporate

Moderates Discord and Roblox, deals with departmental tasks and answers tickets, also hosts training and shifts.

How to obtain

Obtained by being promoted from Junior Corporate.

Head Corporate

Moderates Discord and Roblox mainly, hosts shifts and training, deals with tickets and co-manages their department.

How to obtain

Obtained by being promoted from Senior Corporate.

Director

Moderates Discord and Roblox, manages their department, answers tickets and oversees all staff.

How to obtain

Obtained by being promoted from Head Corporate.

Development Team

Developer

Has no game access and deals with development externally.

How to obtain

Obtained by passing a development application or being contacted by Ownership.

Ownership Team

Vice Chairman

Manages all company-related issues and oversees operations and Corporate Team.

How to obtain

Obtained by being selected by the Chairman.

Manages all company operations and development.

How to obtain

This rank is unobtainable.
